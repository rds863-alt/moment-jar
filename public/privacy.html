Moment Jar

Privacy policy

Effective September 8, 2026

What we store

Each moment you add — its text, mood, tag, and date — is stored in a Supabase database hosted in the United States. Your moments are private to your account: the database is configured so that only the signed-in owner can read or change their own rows.

A copy is also kept in your browser's local storage so the app opens instantly. It is cleared when you sign out.

Your email address

We use your email address for two things only: to send you a sign-in code, and, if you turn them on in Settings, the optional daily reminder and weekly recap emails. You can turn either off at any time in Settings.

Who delivers email

Sign-in codes, reminders, and recaps are delivered by Resend, an email delivery service. Resend receives your address and the message content in order to deliver it.

What we don't do

There are no ads. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with anyone for marketing. Moment Jar does not use advertising trackers.

Getting your data out

Every completed year can be exported as a PDF book from the home screen ("Make a printable book"). That file contains every moment from that year.

Deleting your account

Email support@harelin.com from the address you sign in with and we will delete your account and every moment in it.

Questions

Write to support@harelin.com.
